# You Do Not Negotiate With Apps. You Accept Private Law.

The modern internet is full of contracts people cannot read, cannot negotiate, and cannot realistically refuse.

Terms of service define what users can do, what can be deleted, how data can be used, what disputes mean, whether arbitration applies, and when access can vanish.

## Private Law

Terms can control:

- data use
- account termination
- content rules
- business models
- dispute process
- arbitration
- API access
- app behavior
- export rights

## Interactive Demo

Terms consequence scanner: select clauses such as data use, arbitration, unilateral changes, API limits, and account termination. The demo translates them into user-facing consequences.

## Main Lesson

Consent is weak when the contract is unreadable, non-negotiable, and required for ordinary life.

## EdgeRun Seed

Open protocols, portable data, and replaceable apps reduce the power of private law because users can leave without losing identity and history.
