# Whoever Controls Discovery Controls Reality

The internet did not only centralize storage. It centralized finding.

Search engines, app store rankings, social feeds, recommendation systems, marketplace placement, ads, SEO, and shadowbans decide what people can see.

## Discovery Power

Discovery controls:

- which apps are installed
- which creators survive
- which merchants get traffic
- which facts are visible
- which communities grow
- which products are trusted

## Interactive Demo

Ranking pressure map: adjust search, ads, recommendation ranking, and shadowban switches. Watch the same content become visible or invisible.

## Main Lesson

Open protocols do not solve discovery by themselves. If finding is centralized, markets and attention remain centralized.

## EdgeRun Seed

Discovery should be plural: local indexes, community indexes, signed catalogs, user-chosen rankers, and transparent reputation sources.
