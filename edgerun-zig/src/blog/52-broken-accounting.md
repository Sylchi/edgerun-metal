# The Internet Is Not Free. It Is Bad At Showing Who Pays.

The internet is not free. It is just bad at showing who pays and who profits.

Users provide data but do not get paid. Users provide attention but do not see the invoice. Users buy devices, internet, electricity, and subscriptions, then get farmed again by ads and tracking. Their devices sit idle while apps outsource work to datacenters. Their social graphs create value, but platforms own the graph.

This is broken accounting.

## What The Current Model Hides

- users provide data
- users provide attention
- users buy devices
- users pay for internet
- users pay for electricity
- users pay subscriptions
- apps consume storage, bandwidth, battery, permissions, and identity
- platforms capture distribution
- cloud providers sell compute while local devices sit idle

The cost exists. It is just routed through dependency, tracking, lock-in, and invisible extraction.

## What Edgerun Accounts For

Every meaningful resource exchange should be explicit:

- I used relay bandwidth, so the relay gets a receipt.
- I stored data on your node, so storage gets a receipt.
- I asked for compute, so the compute node gets a receipt.
- I authorized access, so the capability proves scope.
- I sent a file, so the hash proves what was shared.
- I consumed more than I contributed, so I pay the difference.
- I contributed more than I consumed, so I earn.

That is not just economics. It is justice at the protocol layer.

## No Hidden Extraction

No freeloading. No lock-in. No hidden extraction.

A fair network cannot be built on vibes. It needs receipts.

Without accounting, open systems get abused by spam, free riders, storage abuse, relay abuse, compute theft, Sybil attacks, fake work, platform capture, and subsidy collapse.

With correct accounting, the system can be generous without being stupid.

## Free Does Not Mean Unmetered

Free means you are not charged rent for what your own devices can already do.

You should not pay to exist online. You should pay only when you ask others to carry your load.

Imagine a village where everyone has tools. If you cook your own food, you do not pay a restaurant. If your neighbor delivers something for you, they deserve compensation. If you store boxes in someone else's garage, they deserve compensation. If you use more electricity than you produce, you pay the grid. If you produce more than you use, you can sell it.

Digital infrastructure should work the same way.

Your device can store, compute, encrypt, sign, render, and communicate. So why are you paying rent to companies for every tiny action?

Because the current internet does not account for resources correctly.

## Accounting Is Accountability

Resource accounting and moral accountability ask the same questions:

- Who did what?
- Who consumed what?
- Who provided what?
- Who authorized what?
- Who owes what?
- Who can prove it?

That is why signed events, sealed messages, receipts, and content hashes are not crypto decoration. They are the accounting system.
