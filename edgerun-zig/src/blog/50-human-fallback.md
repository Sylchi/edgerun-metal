# Automation Is Great Until There Is No Human Left To Appeal To

A system with no appeal path is not efficient. It is brittle.

Automated systems remove humans for speed and cost. That works until an account is banned, a bank freezes funds, delivery support loops forever, identity verification fails, a SIM is lost, or a platform locks someone out.

## Failure Without Appeal

- account ban
- bank freeze
- SIM loss
- delivery dispute
- identity check failure
- payment block
- app store rejection
- fraud score mistake

## Interactive Demo

Fallback path audit: choose a failure and see whether the service offers web access, human support, physical fallback, portable identity, or only an automated loop.

## Main Lesson

Critical systems need appeal paths. Removing all humans can turn ordinary edge cases into exclusion.

## EdgeRun Seed

User-owned identity, exportable data, local-first storage, and signed receipts give people evidence and alternative paths when automation fails.
