# Before the Internet: Your Device Could Already Do Most of This

Your phone can edit 4K video, but your notes app asks a server for permission to show you text.

Most daily app workloads are small. Messages, notes, contacts, calendars, local search, small databases, simple automation, and many assistant tasks fit comfortably on the device the user already owns.

## Purpose

Destroy the myth that every useful service needs the cloud as its owner. The cloud can be useful capacity. It should not automatically become the place where identity, memory, and permission live.

## Visual idea

What you do: send text, store photos, search notes, sync contacts, ask simple questions.

What your device can do: billions of operations per second, local storage, encryption, networking, graphics, and AI inference.

## Interactive demo

A workload estimator lets the reader select messages per day, photos, notes, contacts, calendar, and a simple local assistant. The page shows how much of the selected work could run locally.

## Main lesson

The cloud is useful, but it should not be the default owner of your life.

## EdgeRun seed
